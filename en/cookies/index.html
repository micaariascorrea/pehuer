Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24/09/2026

This policy explains how www.pehuer.com (the “Site”), operated by PEHUER SPACE, uses cookies and similar technologies. It complements our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies such as localStorage work in a comparable way. In this policy we use “cookies” for all of them.

2. What we use

Strictly necessary and preferences. They let the Site work and remember choices such as language (Spanish or English). They do not require consent because without them the Site cannot provide the service you asked for.

NameTypePurposeDuration
pehuer-lang localStorage (first-party) Remember the selected language Until you delete it

Third parties. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. The lunar globe and the rest of the interface are served from our hosting. We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies.

When you send the Join form, the message is transmitted through FormSubmit so it reaches info@pehuer.com. That happens only when you submit it.

3. How to manage cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). If you block local storage, the Site may not remember the selected language.

4. Changes

We may update this policy if the cookies we use change. The current version will always be on this page.
